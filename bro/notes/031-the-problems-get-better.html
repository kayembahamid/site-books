Find Your Way Into The Real World

The Problems Get Better

Note 031 of 62 · 207 words · Find Your Way Into The Real World

If problems never stop, maybe zero problems was never the goal.

What if the goal is better ones?

Problems that come from growth. From responsibility. From building something. From becoming someone stronger than you were.

That took me years to understand.

When you're young it's easy to think successful people have fewer problems. The older you get, the less true that looks.

No money comes with money problems. Money comes with different money problems. Looking for a job has job problems. Having a job has workplace problems. Managers have people problems. Business owners have business problems. Parents have parent problems.

They don't disappear. They change.

The goal was never zero problems. It's learning which ones are worth carrying.

Some come from staying stuck. Some from avoiding responsibility. Some from refusing to grow.

But some arrive because your life is expanding. You're carrying more. Building more. Responsible for more than yourself.

Some of the hardest stretches of my life were the ones where I learned the most. Not because they were easy. Because they forced me to adapt.

Problems are usually a sign something is changing.

The goal is becoming the kind of person who can handle bigger ones.

Success doesn't remove problems. It gives you better ones.
