What the program is

Three things every student gets.

01

Personalised roadmap

We start from where the student actually is, not from where a syllabus says they should be. The roadmap gets rewritten as they move.

02

Real projects

Something that works, that they built, that they can show someone. Not exercises that get deleted at the end of the session.

03

Safety-first mindset

Security is taught next to the code, not after it. How accounts get taken, why input is never trusted, what a footprint costs.

Who it is for

Students who have outgrown the tutorials, and teachers who were handed a curriculum.

Some students arrive having finished every free course they could find and with no idea what comes next. Some arrive having never written a line. Both work. Educators are welcome to apply for themselves: several of the five spots each term go to teachers preparing to run a computing class for the first time.

How to join

Apply, talk, start.

  1. Fill in the application form. It takes about five minutes and asks what the student has already tried.
  2. We talk. A short call with the student and, if they are under thirteen, a parent.
  3. If it fits, we agree a roadmap and a schedule. If it does not, you get an honest answer and a suggestion of what would fit better.
