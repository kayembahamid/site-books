About this book

A teenager does not need a gentler version of a programming book. They need one that respects what they already do online and builds from there.

This book teaches the language properly and refuses to separate coding from safety. The chapter on forms and input is also the chapter on why input is never trusted. The chapter on accounts is also the chapter on passwords.
