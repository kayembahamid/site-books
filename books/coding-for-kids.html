About this book

Most coding books for children assume there is a programmer in the house. This one does not. Every idea arrives in plain language first, gets a paper exercise second, and only then goes near a keyboard.

A child who finishes it can read a short program, say out loud what it does, change it on purpose, and fix it when it breaks. That is the whole goal. Not fluency in a language, but the habit of thinking in steps.

What is inside

  1. What a computer is actually doing
  2. Instructions, in order, every time
  3. Your first program on paper
  4. Loops: saying it once instead of ten times
  5. Choices: if this, then that
  6. Keeping things in boxes (variables)
  7. When it breaks, and why that is fine
  8. Drawing with code
  9. Making something move
  10. Your own small project
