About this book

This is the book for the adult standing at the front of the room who was handed a computing curriculum and no training.

Every lesson comes with what to say, what students usually get wrong, how long it really takes, and what to do when half the class finishes early. Answer keys for every exercise in the other three books are in the back.

  2. Scope and sequence, one year
